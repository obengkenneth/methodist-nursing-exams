## 2. Creating an administrator account (one-time setup)

- **When to use:** For creating admins
- **Steps:**
  1. In your browser, go to the **Setup** page (your institution will provide the link, or use the link that goes to "Administrator Setup").
  2. Enter the **Setup Key** provided ( **MUG-NURSING-SETUP-2024**).
  3. Enter **Full Name**, **Email**, and **Password** for the  administrator.
  4. Click **Create Admin Account**.
  5. When you see "Admin account created. You can now log in," you will be taken to the **Sign in** page after a few seconds.
  6. Sign in with the same **email** and **password**; you will then see the **Admin** area (overview and sidebar with Manage Tests, Manage Students, Student Results).
- **Note:** Keep the setup key and admin credentials secure. After the  admin is created, students are created only by administrators from the admin area.

## 3. Student guide

### Signing in

- Open the portal and click **Sign in** (or go to the Sign in page).
- Enter the **email** and **password** given to you by your department or administrator.
- After signing in, you will see the **Student Dashboard**.

### Student navigation (sidebar)

- **Dashboard** — overview and exam cards.
- **Available Tests** — list of exams you can take.
- **My Results** — list of your completed exams and scores.

### Dashboard and available tests

- **Dashboard** shows an overview; **Available Tests** shows the same set of exam cards.
- Each card shows: exam title, description, duration, optional image, **Start Exam** (or **No questions yet** if the test has no questions), and for completed tests: score/percentage and pass/fail.
- Click **Start Exam** to open the exam (only enabled when the test has questions and, if the exam does not allow retakes, you have not already taken it).

### Taking an exam

- When you start an exam, a **timer** counts down. When time runs out, the exam may be submitted automatically.
- You see **one question at a time**. Choose one answer: **A**, **B**, **C**, or **D**, then use **Previous** or **Next** to move between questions.
- You can **flag** questions to review them later (optional).
- When finished, click **Submit** and confirm. You will then be able to see your results from **My Results**.

### Viewing results

- From **My Results**, click a completed exam to open its **result detail**.
- You will see your **score**, **percentage**, **pass/fail**, and **time spent**. Below that is the list of **questions and your answers**.
- Use the tabs to filter by **All**, **Correct**, **Incorrect**, or **Flagged**.
- Click a question to expand it and see the question text, your chosen answer, the correct answer, and any rationale.
- Use **Print Results** or **Download PDF** to get a clean copy of your score and question-by-question review for your records.

## 4. Administrator guide

### Signing in

- Use the same **Sign in** page. After signing in with an administrator account, you will see the **Admin** area.

### Admin navigation (sidebar)

- **Overview** — summary numbers and quick links.
- **Manage Tests** — create and edit exams.
- **Manage Students** — create and manage student accounts.
- **Student Results** — view all exam submissions.

### Overview

- The Overview page shows summary cards: **Total Tests**, **Registered Students**, **Test Submissions**, **Total Questions**. It also has short **Quick Actions** that point you to Manage Tests, Manage Students, and Student Results.

### Manage Tests

- You see a list of all tests and can search by title. Use **Create** to add a new test or **Edit** on a row to change it.
- For each test you can set: **Title**, **Description**, **Duration** (minutes), **Allow retake** (yes/no), **Passing percentage**, **Status** (draft / active / completed), and optional **Department**.
- Each test has an option to open **Questions** — use this to add, edit, or remove the questions for that exam. Only **active** tests that have at least one question appear for students and can be started.

### Manage questions (for a test)

- From **Manage Tests**, click **Questions** on a test to open the **Manage Questions** page for that exam. A breadcrumb at the top shows Admin → Manage Tests → [exam name].
- You can **add**, **edit**, or **delete** questions. For each question you enter: the **question text**, the four options **A**, **B**, **C**, **D(optional)**, which option is **correct**, an optional **rationale**, and **marks**.

### Manage Students

- This page lists all **registered students**. You can **create** a new student by entering **Full name**, **Email**, optional **Student ID**, and **Password**. You can **edit** a student's name, email, or student ID, **activate or deactivate** their account (so they can or cannot sign in), and **change** their password.

### Student Results

- This page lists **all exam submissions**: student name, test title, score, percentage, pass/fail, and date. Use the **search** box to filter by student name or test title.
- Click a row to open the **result detail** for that submission. You will see the same kind of view as students (score, time, questions and answers, correct/incorrect) for review or support.

## 5. Tips

- **Collapsing the sidebar (tablets/desktop):** Use the **menu icon** in the top header to collapse or expand the sidebar. When collapsed, the logo and menu icons stay visible.
- **Signing out:** Click your **name and email** in the top-right (pill-shaped button) to open the menu, then choose **Sign out**.
- **Deactivated students:** If you deactivate a student, they will no longer be able to sign in and may see a message that their account has been deactivated.
